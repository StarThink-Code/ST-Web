Our story

Born in 2025, built to do things differently.

StarThink was born in 2025 when Syazmeer Khan and Sharon Seah decided it was time to do things differently. They saw too many businesses struggling with ads and content that failed to connect. Syazmeer brought his marketing expertise, Sharon brought her sales and people skills, and together they built StarThink — a place where creativity and strategy work hand in hand.

What started as a small team quickly grew into a movement, helping brands tell better stories, run smarter campaigns, and grow with purpose.

This is just the beginning. At StarThink, we believe the only limit is your imagination — and we are here to help brands fly.
